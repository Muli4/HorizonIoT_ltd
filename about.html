HOW OUR TELEMATICS SOLUTIONS EASE IT FOR YOU

Enhanced Fleet Visibility

  • Real-Time Tracking: Monitor vehicle locations in real time, ensuring you always know where your assets are.
  • Historical Data: Access historical routes and usage patterns to make informed decisions.
  • Help improve fleet safety through continuous recording, advanced machine vision, and risky driving event tracking — giving clients a streamlined and unified experience.

Safety and Compliance

  • Driver Behavior Monitoring: Track driving habits (speeding, harsh braking) to promote safer driving and reduce accident risk.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Ensure compliance with transport regulations through accurate reporting and documentation.

Improved Efficiency

  • Route Optimization: Utilize smart routing to reduce travel times and fuel consumption.
  • Reduced Downtime: Predictive maintenance alerts help prevent unexpected breakdowns, keeping your fleet operational.

Fuel Management

  • Fuel Level Sensors continuously detect fuel levels by measuring capacitance variations as the level changes.
  • Ideal for anti-siphon, fuel-theft detection, and fleet management applications.
  • Note: Not for measuring water or conductive solutions.

Cold Chain Management

  • Temperature Monitoring: Real-time tracking of cargo temperature and humidity to maintain product integrity.
  • Alerts and Reports: Instant notifications for temperature deviations and detailed audit reports for compliance.
  • Smart Sensors: Seamless integration with IoT temperature sensors for refrigerated trucks and cold storage units.

Driver Scorecard

  • Performance Insights: Track and rank drivers based on safety, efficiency, and compliance metrics.
  • Behavior Analytics: Analyze events like speeding, harsh braking, and idling to improve overall performance.
  • Incentive Programs: Motivate drivers through performance-based rewards and continuous feedback.

GENSET Monitoring System

  • Comprehensive Monitoring: Track your genset fleet 24/7 from a unified platform.
  • Real-Time Data: Access 400+ parameters including power output, fuel levels, and engine performance.
  • Smart Alerts: Receive instant SMS and email notifications for critical events such as fuel theft or engine start/stop.
  • Analytics & Reports: Generate detailed performance and fuel efficiency reports for optimized operations.

Integration with Other Systems

  • Seamless Connectivity: Easily integrate with other fleet management tools, ERP systems, and analytics platforms.
  • Unified Dashboard: Get a comprehensive view of fleet operations across all systems.
  • Data Sync: Ensure consistent data sharing between telematics and third-party applications.

Remote Access

  • 24/7 Monitoring: Access live video feeds and telematics data anytime, anywhere.
  • Quick Response: Take immediate action in case of incidents or operational issues.
  • Secure Cloud Access: Manage and review fleet activities through encrypted remote connections.
