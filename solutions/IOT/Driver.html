Driver Management

Empower your fleet operations with real-time visibility into driver performance, compliance, and safety. Horizon IoT’s Driver Management Solution ensures responsible driving, improved productivity, and lower operational costs.

At Horizon IoT Ltd, our Driver Management Solution combines IoT, telematics, and AI to monitor driver behavior in real time. Gain complete visibility into performance metrics such as speeding, harsh braking, fatigue, and route adherence.

This powerful tool helps fleet managers improve accountability, reduce risks, and encourage safe driving practices. Whether managing logistics, passenger transport, or delivery fleets, Horizon IoT gives you actionable insights to make better operational decisions.

Why Choose Our Driver Management Solution

Our advanced telematics and analytics tools provide the insights you need to promote safety, reduce costs, and improve driver performance across your fleet.

Driver Scoring & Behavior Monitoring

Track driver behavior patterns such as speeding, idling, and harsh braking to enhance safety and fuel efficiency.

Compliance & License Management

Ensure all drivers meet compliance standards with automatic reminders for license renewals, certifications, and rest schedules.

Performance Analytics

Leverage data-driven insights to evaluate driver performance, improve scheduling, and reduce operational risks.

Integration with Fleet Systems

Seamlessly connect driver analytics with GPS tracking, fuel monitoring, and video telematics for a unified fleet management view.

How Our Driver Management System Works

Real-Time Monitoring

  • Live Insights: View driver behavior and route progress in real time.
  • Incident Alerts: Receive notifications for unsafe or non-compliant driving behavior.

Safety & Compliance

  • Policy Enforcement: Ensure adherence to road safety and operational policies.
  • Automated Reports: Maintain audit-ready driver compliance reports.

Performance Metrics

  • Driver Scoring: Automatically rank drivers based on performance indicators.
  • Efficiency Tracking: Identify training needs and reward safe behavior.

Video Telematics Integration

  • Camera Monitoring: Pair with in-cabin cameras to record and analyze driver behavior.
  • AI Analytics: Detect fatigue, distractions, and violations in real time.
