Video Telematics

Empower your fleet with AI-driven dashcams that combine live video and telematics data to enhance safety, accountability, and operational visibility.

Horizon IoT Ltd provides an advanced Video Telematics Solution that integrates real-time video footage with GPS and vehicle data to improve fleet safety and performance. Our system leverages AI analytics to detect unsafe driving behavior, provide visual evidence of incidents, and optimize operational control.

Whether managing commercial fleets or logistics operations, our intelligent video solution helps you protect assets, train drivers, and reduce risks through visual insights and automatic event detection.

Why Choose Our Video Telematics

Gain full visibility into every journey with AI-powered monitoring, real-time alerts, and high-definition video evidence.

Real-time Video Streaming

Monitor your fleet live through 4G-connected dashcams with instant access to road and cabin views.

AI Driver Behavior Detection

Detect fatigue, distraction, and harsh driving using AI algorithms that trigger immediate safety alerts.

Event-Triggered Video Alerts

Automatically record and upload clips during incidents like speeding, collisions, or unauthorized use.

Cloud Storage & Evidence Playback

Securely store and access historical footage for investigations, insurance claims, and compliance reporting.

How Our Video Telematics System Works

Live Video Capture

  • Dual Dashcams: Record both road and cabin views for comprehensive visibility.
  • HD Recording: Capture clear visuals under all lighting and weather conditions.

AI-Powered Analytics

  • Behavior Detection: Identify risky behaviors like distraction or drowsiness in real time.
  • Incident Insights: Review automated event logs and video clips for driver performance analysis.

Instant Alerts

  • Smart Notifications: Receive push alerts for events such as harsh braking or collisions.
  • Evidence Sharing: Instantly access and share footage with managers or authorities.

Cloud Storage & Access

  • Secure Backups: Store video and telematics data safely in the cloud.
  • Remote Playback: Review footage from any device, anytime, through a web platform.

Fleet Performance Insights

  • Visual Reports: Analyze trends in driver performance and route safety.
  • Operational Control: Improve accountability and reduce accident-related costs.

Seamless Integration

  • Platform Compatibility: Integrates with leading telematics platforms and fleet systems.
  • Custom APIs: Extend functionality through flexible video and data interfaces.
