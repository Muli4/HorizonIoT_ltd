Track and Trace Solution

Real-time tracking and intelligent visibility for all your assets and vehicles.

Horizon IoT Ltd delivers powerful Track and Trace solutions designed to help organizations monitor their fleets, vehicles, and assets in real time. Our system ensures end-to-end visibility, security, and operational control through seamless IoT connectivity.

Whether you manage logistics, delivery services, or enterprise mobility, our real-time GPS tracking technology gives you full insight into movement, performance, and productivity, empowering data-driven decisions that optimize efficiency and reduce costs.

Why Choose Our Track and Trace Solution

Horizon IoT’s Track and Trace Solution provides unmatched fleet visibility, theft prevention, and operational intelligence — ensuring that your assets are always connected, protected, and optimized.

Live GPS Tracking

Monitor your vehicles and assets in real time with precision mapping, geofencing, and instant alerts for movement or unauthorized activity.

Incident Insights

Access replayable route history, trip logs, and event-based tracking to identify issues and improve operational safety.

Operational Efficiency

Boost productivity and reduce downtime through optimized routes, smart dispatching, and performance analytics.

Seamless Integration

Integrate tracking data easily with ERP, CRM, or fleet management platforms for unified operational insights.

How Our Track and Trace System Works

Real-Time Visibility

  • Instant Tracking: See live locations of all your vehicles and assets on an interactive map.
  • Route Playback: Review journey history to analyze travel efficiency and driver behavior.
  • Geofencing: Get alerts when assets enter or exit designated zones.

Security & Compliance

  • Theft Prevention: Detect unauthorized movement and trigger instant alerts for rapid response.
  • Compliance Tracking: Generate audit-ready trip and usage reports to meet regulatory requirements.

Performance Analytics

  • Smart Insights: Analyze trip durations, stop times, and fuel usage for data-driven planning.
  • Efficiency Metrics: Identify underutilized assets and optimize routes for cost savings.

Fleet Optimization

  • Predictive Alerts: Receive notifications for maintenance or route deviations before issues escalate.
  • Integrated Dashboards: View all asset data in one intuitive interface.

Environment Monitoring

  • IoT Sensor Support: Track environmental data such as temperature and humidity for sensitive cargo.
  • Alerts & Records: Ensure delivery quality with continuous monitoring and automated reporting.

Driver Behavior Analytics

  • Behavior Insights: Monitor driving patterns such as speeding and idling to enhance safety.
  • Performance Scoring: Rank drivers and promote accountability with automated reports.
